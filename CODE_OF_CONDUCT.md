# Code of conduct

Everyone taking part in this project, in issues, pull requests, reviews and
discussions, is expected to be considerate and respectful. Disagree with
ideas, not with people. Assume good faith. Keep it on topic.

Not acceptable: harassment, insults, discriminatory remarks, publishing
someone's private information, or sustained disruption. The maintainer may
edit, remove or lock contributions that cross that line, and may block
repeat offenders.

If something happened that you want looked at, write to the maintainer at
the e-mail address on the GitHub profile, or open a private report through
GitHub. Reports are handled in confidence.

This text follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
