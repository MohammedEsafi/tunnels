# Code of conduct

## Expected

- Be considerate and respectful in issues, pull requests, reviews and
  discussions.
- Disagree with ideas, not with people. Assume good faith. Keep it on topic.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults or discriminatory remarks.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Sustained disruption of a thread or a review.

## What happens

1. The maintainer may edit, remove or lock contributions that cross the line.
2. Repeat offenders may be blocked from the repository.

## Reporting

Write to the maintainer at the e-mail address on the GitHub profile, or open a
private report through GitHub. Reports are handled in confidence.

This text follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
